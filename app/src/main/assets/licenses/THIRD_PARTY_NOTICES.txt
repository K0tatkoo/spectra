on synths and hardstyle drums generated by Spectra's training code. The weights
are a work derived from that data and are shared under the strictest of its
terms: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). They may be used, shared
and adapted for non-commercial purposes, with credit, under the same licence.

  https://music.ai/research/
- Slakh2100 (Manilow et al., 2019) — CC BY 4.0
  https://zenodo.org/records/4599666
- MUSDB18-HQ (Rafii et al., 2019) — its vocal stems only; non-commercial use
  https://zenodo.org/records/3338373
